About SatrixNow

SATRIXNOW was established in 2020 in the United Kingdom. It's a global leader in AI-driven cryptocurrency mining and PoS blockchain infrastructure. We are dedicated to making high-performance digital asset mining accessible, efficient, and profitable for everyone.

Founded by a team of blockchain technology experts and fintech veterans, SatrixNow has built a state-of-the-art ecosystem that leverages cutting-edge hardware and proprietary AI optimization technology to deliver consistent results for its growing global community.

Through advanced automation, intelligent mining systems, and decentralized infrastructure, SatrixAI continues to redefine the future of digital finance and blockchain participation.

By 2025, SatrixAi successfully expanded its digital infrastructure footprint into emerging markets across Africa, prioritizing sustainable energy solutions and decentralized computing power. This expansion reflects the company’s commitment to innovation, accessibility, and global financial inclusion.

SatrixAi believes in building a stronger foundation for a more inclusive, decentralized, and technologically advanced financial ecosystem where individuals worldwide can participate in the future of digital assets.
